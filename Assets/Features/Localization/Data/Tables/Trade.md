# Localization: Trade
Trade strings are mostly shown within the trade UI (i.e. when clicking a good in your inventory or a towns inventory to sell or buy).

## Key Organization
- Trade.Details.*: Shown in the trade UI (i.e. when clicking a good in your inventory or a towns inventory to sell or buy).
- Trade.Failure.*: Shown when a trade fails, for example when trying to sell a good to a town that doesn't want to buy it. These are shown as tooltips/popups over the inventory cell when clicked.
- Trade.Haggling.*: Shown in the haggle UI when trading.
- Trade.Modifiers.*: Different modifiers to prices of goods. They are mostly shown when hovering the price of a good in the TradeUI.
